Our Businesses

Distinct activities. One shared corporate identity.

Gongpe brings together businesses operating across different areas of economic activity. Each business has its own focus, market, and operating direction, supported by the broader vision and development of the group.

BUSINESS UNIT 01

Gongpe Finance

Financial Services & Advisory finance.gongpe.com

Financial precision. Practical governance.

Gongpe Finance focuses on corporate finance, accounting, capital advisory, and financial management activities across business units and enterprise partners.

The unit provides the financial structure, management discipline, and capital allocation frameworks required to support long-term economic growth.

BUSINESS UNIT 02

Gongpe Commerce

Trade & Commercial Operations commerce.gongpe.com

Trade and commercial opportunities.

Gongpe Commerce focuses on commercial activities involving products, physical goods, trading operations, and supply distribution networks.

The unit seeks to facilitate efficient trade routes, product sourcing, and wholesale distribution mechanisms.

BUSINESS UNIT 03

Gongpe Travel

Travel, Tourism & Hospitality travel.gongpe.com

Travel, destinations and experiences.

Gongpe Travel focuses on travel and tourism-related activities, connecting visitors with regional destinations, hospitality services, and curated travel experiences.

The business operates with an emphasis on sustainable tourism, authentic regional engagements, and reliable travel management services.

BUSINESS UNIT 04

Gongpe Entertainment

Media, Content & Events media.gongpe.com

Entertainment, media and creative content.

Gongpe Entertainment focuses on media creation, digital content production, live events, and creative experiences.

It aims to produce engaging media formats and organize events that resonate with diverse local and regional audiences.

BUSINESS UNIT 05

Gongpe Technologies

Technology & Digital Solutions tech.gongpe.com

Building technology infrastructure.

Gongpe Technologies focuses on software development, digital platforms, cloud infrastructure, and technical solutions supporting modern operations.

The unit powers the group's digital transformation initiatives and develops tech products for market needs.

Corporate Identity

About Gongpe

A group built around business, capability, and long-term progress.

Who We Are

Gongpe is a diversified business group being developed with interests across distinct areas of economic activity.

We operate with a long-term perspective: developing businesses, building capabilities and organising resources to create sustainable economic activity. Our objective is to build a group of enterprises that can grow, adapt and contribute lasting value.

Headquartered in Kathmandu, Nepal, Gongpe brings together finance, commerce, travel, media, and technology under a unified corporate identity.

Our Perspective

We believe progress comes through purposeful activity—identifying needs, exploring possibilities, developing solutions, and creating durable value.

Continuous Learning

We view enterprise development as an ongoing process of learning, adapting operating models, and strengthening capabilities for the future.

Responsible Growth

Growth should be supported by sound operational fundamentals, financial discipline, and a commitment to long-term continuity.

Looking Ahead
