About Gongpe

A focused group. Clear operating priorities.

Gongpe is a business group based in Kathmandu, Nepal. It develops and supports practical business activities with a focus on enterprise support and technology solutions.

At present, the group’s active work is concentrated in two practices: Gongpe Consulting for financial and business advisory, and Gongpe Technologies for digital tools, platforms, and information systems.

The group provides a shared foundation while allowing each practice to operate with clear priorities.

Focused activity

Concentrating resources on a small number of practical businesses rather than a wide set of loosely related units.

Operating relevance

Solutions shaped around real information, process, and decision needs in the operating environment.

Longer horizon

Building capabilities that can be sustained, evaluated, and improved over time.
